Verification standard

How American Recon Handles Raw Information

1. FIND THE ORIGINAL Trace every claim back to the report, document, video, dataset or firsthand statement.
2. CHECK THE SOURCE Identify who published it, what evidence is supplied and what interests may affect the account.
3. SEEK CONFIRMATION Look for separate sources that independently support the important details.
4. LABEL UNCERTAINTY Developing, disputed and unverified information must never be presented as confirmed fact.
